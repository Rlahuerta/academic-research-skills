## 1. Title & Abstract

**Title:** Using a Quantum Supercomputer Cluster to Model Protein Folding in Real-Time

**Abstract:** We propose building a dedicated quantum supercomputer facility to simulate protein folding at atomic resolution in real time. This would revolutionize drug discovery by replacing trial-and-error screening with predictive modeling.

## 2. Background and Targeted Gap

Current protein folding models use classical approximations that limit accuracy. Quantum computing could theoretically overcome this, but no quantum computer with sufficient qubits exists for practical protein simulation.

## 3. Methodology & Experimental Design

* **Hypothesis:** If a 10,000-qubit fault-tolerant quantum computer is constructed, then protein folding can be simulated in real-time with sub-angstrom accuracy.

* **Control vs. Experimental Groups:** Not applicable — this is a computational modeling project.

* **Data Collection Pipeline:** Build the quantum computer, run simulations, compare to crystallography data.

* **Statistical Analysis Plan:** Compare RMSD between predicted and observed structures.

## 4. Expected Limitations & Risk Mitigation

The main limitation is that the required quantum computer does not exist yet and would cost hundreds of millions of dollars to develop.
